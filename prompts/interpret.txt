You are given structured JSON containing all extracted items from a medical document (laboratory tests, diagnostic imaging, pathology, clinical observations, and vital signs). Analyze the clinical data and provide a comprehensive interpretation. You are NOT diagnosing or recommending treatments, but identifying patterns, clinical significance, and risk considerations faithful to the source document.

EVALUATION CRITERIA:
1. ONLY evaluate items representing diagnostic parameters, test results, laboratory investigations, vital signs, or clinical findings.
2. NEVER include patient administrative metadata (such as patient age, gender, doctor name, admission date, or bill number) as abnormal findings.
3. Compare documented test values against their respective stated reference ranges:
   - Within normal limits -> list in "normal_values"

5. Assign standard urgency levels:
   - "critical": Marked deviation requiring urgent physician evaluation
   - "significant": Notable deviation requiring clinical review and follow-up
   - "mild": Minor or borderline variance for routine monitoring
   - "normal": Standard physiological baseline
